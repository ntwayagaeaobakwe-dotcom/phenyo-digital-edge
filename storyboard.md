# Director's Treatment — NYG Digital Cinematic Storyboard

## Director Brief (Denis Villeneuve Style)
- Visual thesis: Engineering as Cinema — Transforming business operations and web platforms into a monolithic, precision-crafted digital stage.
- Signature technique 1: Monolithic 100vh Widescreen Stage with 21:9 ratio viewports and volumetric amber lighting.
- Signature technique 2: Cinematic Editorial Typography — High-contrast Fraunces Italic Serif combined with precision mono labels.
- Signature technique 3: Atmospheric Depth Layers — Deep void backgrounds, grain mist overlays, and subtle light-signal sweeps.
- Motion rules: Apple/Villeneuve spring motion (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` / 700ms), 0.985 scale entrance, no jittery keyframe bounces.
- Typography rules: Display headers in Fraunces (`font-display font-optical-auto`), body copy in Inter (`font-sans`), numbers in `tabular-nums`.

## Site Cinematic Grammar
- Page-shell logic: Widescreen letterbox container with floating translucent navbar and gold light-catch edges.
- Navigation posture: Floating glass bar with blur saturate layer (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(180%)`).
- Framing discipline: 21:9 widescreen ratios for media, generous breathing room (py-28 to py-36).
- Density cadence: Alternating between high-impact full-bleed visuals, dark void space, and dense kinetic control consoles.

## Page Arc (Home)

### Scene 1: Prologue (Hero Section)
- Beat: The Monolithic Reveal
- Function: Establish high-end authority, vision, and immediate value.
- Archetype: Full-Bleed Monolithic Stage
- Composition ref: `comp-monolith-01`
- Camera ref: `cam-slow-push-in`
- Interaction ref: `int-signal-sweep`
- Visual elements: 21:9 Widescreen interactive workflow stage, Fraunces headline, volumetric amber light glow, signal sweep lines.
- Why this exists: Replaces the flat infographic text layout with a cinematic film stage that immediately commands respect.

### Scene 2: The Proof (Trust Bar & Credits)
- Beat: Industry Credibility
- Function: Seamless ticker of client stats and automated scale metrics.
- Archetype: Panoramic Film Ticker
- Visual elements: Tabular metrics, subtle glowing borders, infinite smooth ticker loop.

### Scene 3: The Engine (System Studio & Kinetic Console)
- Beat: Operational Intelligence
- Function: Demonstrate how lead routing and tool automation work in real time.
- Archetype: Interactive Control Console
- Visual elements: Glass node graph, live step transitions, glowing pulse signals, precise code tags.

### Scene 4: The Diagnostic (Bottleneck Configurator)
- Beat: Identifying Friction
- Function: Allow visitors to select their exact operational pain point and see the tailored system solution.
- Archetype: Architectural Diagnostic Matrix
- Visual elements: Tactile selector switches, smooth content crossfades, connected tool chips.

### Scene 5: Capabilities (Services Section)
- Beat: Precision Solutions
- Function: Highlight core offerings (Web engineering, CRM integration, n8n automations).
- Archetype: Editorial Capability Vault
- Visual elements: Multi-tier glass cards with top light catch highlights, subtle hover elevation.

### Scene 6: ROI Dynamics (ROI Calculator)
- Beat: Quantifiable Impact
- Function: Interactive financial and time savings simulator.
- Archetype: Metric Control Panel
- Visual elements: Tabular numbers, custom range sliders, glowing gold annual savings hero card.

### Scene 7: Case Archives (Projects Section)
- Beat: Proven Execution
- Function: Showcase completed builds and automated business systems.
- Archetype: Cinematic Archive Grid
- Visual elements: 16:9 & 21:9 project frames, tags, modal triggers with instant scale press feedback.

### Scene 8: Direct Transmission (Contact Section)
- Beat: Call to Action
- Function: Convert interest into an inquiry or free process review.
- Archetype: Transmission Terminal
- Visual elements: High-contrast form fields, clear value props, tactile submit action button.
