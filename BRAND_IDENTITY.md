# NYG Agency — Compact NYG Identity

## Authority

The finalized Compact NYG identity uses the supplied NYG logo artwork, Carbon / Platinum surfaces, Manrope display typography, and Source Sans 3 body typography. This replaces the prior experimental identity direction. The implemented token system and responsive rules are documented in DESIGN.md.

NYG Agency is the public trading brand. The legal company name remains **NYG Digital FZE LLC**; preserve that name in registration and legal disclosures.

## Brand expression

Carbon is the foundation, dark panels create separation, and platinum provides restrained emphasis. Manrope establishes clear geometric headings; Source Sans 3 supports descriptions and forms. Keep the supplied identity prominent, surrounding it with space and flat tonal contrast. Refer to DESIGN.md for exact normative values and component behavior.
