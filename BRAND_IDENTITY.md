# NYG Agency — Luxury Tech Identity

## Direction

NYG Agency is presented as a precise, founder-led technology partner for established businesses, luxury brands, and international clients. The identity uses architectural geometry, restrained platinum, and generous negative space to communicate trust, intelligence, exclusivity, and modern digital capability.

The mark is intentionally flat. Its authority comes from proportion and construction rather than chrome effects, neon, shields, crowns, or generic technology symbols.

## Concept development

### Concept 01 — Interlock (selected)

N, Y, and G share a single structural spine. The N is built from two verticals and a diagonal, the Y branches from the upper joint, and the G closes the lower right with a precise cut. The shared strokes make the symbol feel connected and architectural while keeping each letter legible. It scales cleanly to a favicon because the construction uses bold, continuous strokes.

### Concept 02 — Cutline

The letters are constructed as a squared architectural frame with a diagonal cut through the central negative space. It feels institutional and precise, with strong signage potential. The denser interior makes it slightly less legible at favicon size.

### Concept 03 — Continuous

A single continuous line moves through the N, branches into the Y, and resolves into a compact G. It has the most futuristic character and works well as a motion path, but the continuous gesture is more abstract at small sizes.

The Interlock concept is the strongest direction for memorability, elegance, scalability, B2B credibility, and long-term use. It is now the production mark.

## Palette

| Role | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NYG Ink | `#101311` | Primary dark field and navigation |
| Graphite | `#171A1C` | Light-background mark and dark text |
| Platinum | `#C8CDD0` | Primary metallic-silver mark |
| Soft white | `#F0F2F3` | Wordmark and high-contrast type |
| Stone | `#9EA6AB` | Secondary wordmark and technical labels |
| Signal mint | `#B5DDC7` | Digital product accent in the existing website system |

Platinum should be rendered as a solid color in core identity files. Use subtle material texture only in a controlled presentation mockup; never rely on a gradient for recognition.

## Typography

- **Primary:** Manrope Variable, weights 500–700. Use for the NYG Agency wordmark, navigation, proposals, and interface copy.
- **Technical support:** JetBrains Mono Variable, weights 400–600. Use for system labels, metadata, and technical annotations.

Keep the wordmark uppercase with generous tracking. Do not use decorative or novelty display fonts.

## Clear space and minimum size

Use clear space equal to the height of the monogram's lower G cut on every side. Keep the monogram at least 16px high in digital UI and 8mm high in print. Use the favicon variation below 24px.

## Application guidance

- **Website navigation:** platinum monogram and soft-white wordmark on NYG Ink.
- **Hero and presentations:** use the horizontal lockup with generous breathing room; keep signal mint to small system accents.
- **Business cards and proposals:** use the light-background lockup on white or warm off-white stock, with graphite text.
- **Social avatar:** use the favicon variation centered on NYG Ink.
- **Single-color and embossing:** use the mono SVG with no interior effects or outline substitutions.

The existing website continues to use the legal and service name “NYG Digital” in SEO and company copy. The new NYG Agency lockups are available for the broader brand identity; change the public wordmark only when the business-name transition is intentional.
